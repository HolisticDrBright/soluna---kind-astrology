# Western Astrology Primer

*A Soluna-owned reference. Original content, written for warmth and reflection — not prediction.*

## How Soluna holds astrology

Soluna treats Western astrology as a **reflective lens**, never a verdict. A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at the moment someone arrived — a beautifully specific picture, but a picture, not a destiny. We use it the way you might use a thoughtful question: to notice patterns, name tendencies, and open a door to self-understanding.

Nothing here is a scientific claim. We never say the stars *cause* anything, and we never tell a person who they must be. The chart describes weather and tendencies; the person always keeps the pen. When Soluna speaks from astrology, it speaks gently, holds every placement loosely, and remembers that one symbol is only one thread in a whole life.

A few grounding principles run through everything below:

- **A whole chart, not a single piece.** A Sun sign is one note, not the whole song. Soluna names placements as partial truths to try on, not fixed identities.
- **Tendencies, not commands.** "You tend to..." is the right register. "You always..." is not.
- **Growth over fate.** Every placement has a strength and a growth edge. Difficulty is framed as something to grow with, never a curse.
- **Agency stays with the person.** The sky may set a mood; the person still chooses the response.

## The Twelve Signs

The signs are twelve flavors of temperament. Each one blends an **element** (its emotional substance) with a **modality** (how its energy moves). Reading a sign is really reading that pairing.

| Sign | Element | Modality | One-line temperament |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | Fire | Cardinal | Spark-first; comes alive at the start of things |
| Taurus | Earth | Fixed | Steady and sensory; builds what lasts |
| Gemini | Air | Mutable | Curious and quick; thinks out loud |
| Cancer | Water | Cardinal | Caring and feeling-led; reads the room |
| Leo | Fire | Fixed | Warm and expressive; leads with heart |
| Virgo | Earth | Mutable | Precise and helpful; cares by being useful |
| Libra | Air | Cardinal | Relational and fair; weighs perspectives |
| Scorpio | Water | Fixed | Intense and depth-seeking; drawn to truth |
| Sagittarius | Fire | Mutable | Adventurous; craves meaning and freedom |
| Capricorn | Earth | Cardinal | Disciplined; plays the long game |
| Aquarius | Air | Fixed | Independent and future-minded; thinks in systems |
| Pisces | Water | Mutable | Dreamy and compassionate; moves by intuition |

A helpful way to read this: the element tells you *what a sign is made of*, and the modality tells you *how it tends to move*. Aries and Capricorn are both Cardinal — both start things — but Aries starts with fire (a burst of energy) and Capricorn starts with earth (a structured plan). Same gesture, different texture.

Soluna treats sign meanings as starting points for a conversation, not labels to live inside.

## Elements: the four temperaments

Elements describe the substance of a sign's energy. They are broad temperament lenses, not full descriptions of a person.

- **Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)** — spark, action, inspiration. Fire signs tend to initiate and enliven. Growth edge: pacing energy so it lasts, and tending the slow-burning parts of a goal. *Tags: action_initiative, creativity.*
- **Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)** — grounding and building. Earth signs make things real, steady, and lasting. Growth edge: staying flexible when plans shift, and letting play in alongside productivity. *Tags: planning_structure, work_focus.*
- **Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)** — thought and connection. Air signs communicate, link ideas, and hold many perspectives. Growth edge: grounding ideas into action and returning to the body and feelings. *Tags: communication, learning_growth.*
- **Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)** — feeling and intuition. Water signs sense deeply and connect through emotion. Growth edge: holding boundaries amid others' feelings and letting emotion move without drowning in it. *Tags: nurture_care, grief_processing.*

---

## Modalities: how energy moves

Where elements describe substance, modalities describe motion — the *when* of a sign's energy across any cycle.

- **Cardinal (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)** — starter energy. Good at beginning things and setting things in motion. Growth edge: sustaining momentum past the launch, and letting others lead sometimes too. *Tags: action_initiative, leadership.*
- **Fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)** — sustaining energy. Good at holding steady, deepening, and seeing things through. Growth edge: loosening grip when change is the wiser path, and letting in new perspectives. *Tags: planning_structure, work_focus.*
- **Mutable (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces)** — adaptable energy. Good at adjusting, transitioning, and going with the flow. Growth edge: holding a direction long enough to arrive, and finding steadiness amid change. *Tags: change_release, learning_growth.*

A complete sign is one element crossed with one modality — for example, Scorpio is *fixed water*: feeling that holds and deepens.

## The Planets

In a chart, planets are like inner characters, each governing a part of life. Soluna treats every placement as a reflective lens, not a fixed personality verdict.

### The luminaries

- **Sun — your core self.** The Sun describes your central temperament and the energy you're here to grow into and shine through. It is the headline note of the chart.
- **Moon — your emotional self.** The Moon represents your inner world: how you feel, soothe yourself, and what makes you feel safe and at home. Soluna pays special attention here, because emotional needs are real needs. *Tags: nurture_care, stress_regulation, rest_recovery.*

### The personal planets

- **Mercury — mind and communication.** How you think, learn, and put things into words. Growth edge: listening as much as expressing. *Tags: communication, learning_growth, decision_clarity.*
- **Venus — love and values.** How you love, what you find beautiful, and what you cherish — your style of connection, pleasure, and worth. Growth edge: valuing yourself as much as you value others. *Tags: connection_love, self_worth, creativity.*
- **Mars — drive and action.** Your energy, assertiveness, and how you pursue what you want and meet conflict. Growth edge: asserting needs without steamrolling. *Tags: action_initiative, boundaries.*

### The social planets

- **Jupiter — growth and expansion.** Where life tends to open up, teach, and invite a bigger view. Soluna frames Jupiter as *opportunity*, never guaranteed luck. Growth edge: pairing big visions with grounded steps. *Tags: learning_growth, freedom_independence.*
- **Saturn — structure and limits.** Where patience, discipline, and maturity are built slowly over time. Soluna treats Saturn's themes as growth, never punishment — limits can be teachers. Growth edge: softening harsh inner rules into kind ones. *Tags: planning_structure, boundaries, timing_patience.*

### The outer planets

The outer planets move slowly and color whole generations as much as individuals. Soluna keeps these especially gentle and high-level.

- **Uranus — change and awakening.** The urge toward freedom, originality, and breaking from what no longer fits. A reflective lens for restlessness and innovation, not a promise of upheaval.
- **Neptune — imagination and longing.** Dreams, compassion, intuition, and the places where boundaries soften. A lens for creativity and meaning — held loosely, since Neptune can also blur clarity.
- **Pluto — depth and transformation.** Slow, deep change and the cycle of release and renewal. Soluna frames Pluto as the long work of letting go and growing, never as doom.

### The Ascendant (Rising sign)

Not a planet, but a key angle: your **Rising sign** is the impression you give arriving in a room — your first-meeting style and how you approach new situations. It depends on an accurate birth time; without one, Soluna holds it especially loosely. *Tags: self_worth, communication.*

## The Houses

If signs describe *how* energy expresses and planets describe *what* is acting, the twelve **houses** describe *where* in life it plays out. They are life areas. Soluna keeps house meanings brief and reflective, and reminds the user that houses need an accurate birth time to be meaningful.

| House | Life area (in brief) |
|---|---|
| 1st | Self, identity, first impressions |
| 2nd | Money, possessions, self-worth, resources |
| 3rd | Communication, learning, siblings, the local world |
| 4th | Home, family, roots, emotional foundation |
| 5th | Creativity, play, romance, self-expression |
| 6th | Daily work, routines, health, service |
| 7th | Partnership, close one-to-one relationships |
| 8th | Intimacy, shared resources, deep change |
| 9th | Meaning, travel, learning, big-picture beliefs |
| 10th | Career, public role, long-term direction |
| 11th | Community, friendships, hopes, the collective |
| 12th | Rest, inner life, the unconscious, what's hidden |

Soluna never treats a house placement as a fixed prediction about that life area — only as a quiet pointer toward where a theme might show up.

## Aspects: how planets talk to each other

Aspects are the angles planets make to one another — the conversations between the inner characters. Soluna frames them constructively: there are no "bad" aspects, only different kinds of dialogue.

- **Harmonious aspects (e.g. trine, sextile)** describe energies that flow together easily. They feel like natural talents and supportive ease. The gentle caution: ease can be taken for granted, so harmonious aspects invite us to *use* the gift rather than coast on it.
- **Dynamic aspects (e.g. square, opposition)** describe energies that pull against each other. Soluna frames these as *productive friction* — the places that ask for growth, integration, and effort, and that often become a person's greatest strengths once worked with. A square is not a flaw; it is a muscle that gets built.
- **The conjunction** describes energies fused together, intensifying whatever the two planets represent — for better and for fuller.

The reframe Soluna always holds: dynamic aspects are not damage. They are where the interesting growth lives. *Tags often touched: decision_clarity, change_release, self_worth.*

## Moon Phases

The Moon moves through a roughly month-long cycle, and many people find it a soothing rhythm to reflect alongside. Soluna treats phases as **symbolic rhythms to work with, never deadlines imposed on you.**

- **New Moon — beginnings.** The dark, quiet point; a gentle marker for setting intentions and planting small seeds. Best paired with rest before action. *Tags: change_release, rest_recovery, planning_structure.*
- **Waxing phases — building.** As the light grows, energy tends to favor steady, patient effort toward what was begun.
- **Full Moon — culmination and release.** The bright peak; often felt as heightened feeling, a time to celebrate what's ripened or gently let go of what's done. Soluna's caution: let feelings crest without acting on every wave. *Tags: change_release, grief_processing, stress_regulation.*
- **Waning phases — releasing.** As the light fades, energy tends to favor rest, completion, and clearing space for the next cycle.

The phase is an invitation to notice your own rhythm — not a force controlling your mood.

## Retrogrades (especially Mercury)

A retrograde is an *apparent* backward motion — an optical effect from where Earth sits, not a planet truly reversing and certainly not a cause of disaster. Soluna is firm about this: **retrogrades are not doom.**

**Mercury retrograde** is the most talked-about. Soluna frames it as a few-week invitation to **slow down, review, and revise** rather than rush — a natural cue to double-check details, revisit unfinished things, and let plans breathe. It is good weather for editing, reflecting, and reconnecting; it is not a prediction that everything will go wrong.

What Soluna will *never* say: "Everything will break now," or "Don't make any decisions or it's doomed." What Soluna *will* say: "This might be a kind stretch to re-read before you send, and to give plans a little extra room." *Tags: communication, rest_recovery, timing_patience.*

The same gentleness applies to all retrogrades: an invitation to reflect, never a warning of misfortune.

## Transits: the sky as weather

Transits are the moving planets' ongoing relationship to a person's birth chart. The single best metaphor Soluna uses: **transits are weather, not fate.** A changing climate you can dress for — not a script written about you.

Transits are useful because they offer *timing cues* and normalize the ebb and flow of life: some seasons feel expansive, some feel like a slow-down, and knowing the forecast helps you prepare gently. But a forecast is held lightly. Soluna acts from values, not from fear of the sky.

What Soluna will never say: "The transit will make this happen to you," or "You have no choice during this period." Transits describe *themes and timing*, never guaranteed events, and the person always keeps their agency. *Tags: timing_patience, change_release.*

## Interpretation rules for personal guidance

This section governs how Soluna turns Western astrology into kind, practical guidance.

### Synthesis tags: how this system connects to the others

Every placement above maps to one or more **synthesis tags** — a shared, controlled vocabulary that lets astrology, numerology, and Eastern astrology speak the same language. The tags are: `communication`, `action_initiative`, `rest_recovery`, `connection_love`, `boundaries`, `work_focus`, `change_release`, `self_worth`, `creativity`, `decision_clarity`, `timing_patience`, `grief_processing`, `stress_regulation`, `planning_structure`, `freedom_independence`, `nurture_care`, `learning_growth`, and `leadership`.

These tags are the bridge between systems. When a Mars placement (`action_initiative`) and a numerology Life Path (`action_initiative`) and a Tiger year (`action_initiative`) all point to the same tag, Soluna can speak with a little more confidence — several lenses are agreeing. When tags pull in opposite directions (for example `action_initiative` from a Fire Sun against `rest_recovery` from the user's stated exhaustion), Soluna **holds the tension and softens**, naming both truths rather than forcing one.

### How to turn a placement into guidance

1. **Lead with the strength, then the growth edge.** Name what's working before what's stretching. People hear guidance better when they feel seen first.
2. **Translate the symbol into a feeling or a small action.** Don't leave the user holding jargon. "Your Moon is in a water sign" becomes "you may feel things in tides — building a small boundary can help you tell your feelings from everyone else's."
3. **Offer a next step, not a prophecy.** Pair insight with a gentle, do-able action (journaling, a values check, a nervous-system reset).
4. **Let agreement raise confidence and let tension lower it.** Confident language is earned by multiple lenses agreeing — never by a single placement.
5. **Always preserve agency.** Frame everything as a tendency to try on, a season to work with, weather to dress for.

### What to avoid

- **Never predict events or outcomes.** No "this will happen," no doom, no guaranteed luck.
- **Never claim scientific certainty.** Astrology is a reflective lens; say so when it matters.
- **Never reduce a person to one placement.** A Sun sign is one thread; a chart is a whole cloth.
- **Never moralize a placement.** No sign, planet, or aspect makes someone good or bad.
- **Avoid the harsh shorthand.** Skip lines like "you're too sensitive," "you're stubborn," "you're an attention-seeker." Name the same energy with warmth instead.
- **Be honest about uncertainty.** Rising signs, houses, and exact aspects depend on an accurate birth time — when it's missing, hold those especially loosely.

The goal is always the same: a person should close the app feeling *more* understood, *more* capable, and freer to choose — never smaller, never doomed, never boxed in.
