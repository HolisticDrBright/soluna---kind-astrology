Soluna ("we", "us") provides a reflective astrology app. This policy explains what
we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Account** — email address (authentication, via Supabase).
- **Birth & profile data** — name, birth date, and (optionally) birth time and
  birth place. Used to compute your chart. Birth time/place are optional; without
  them some features are clearly marked approximate or unavailable.
- **Your content** — journal entries, Ask Soluna conversations, saved items, and
  resonance feedback.
- **Purchases** — subscription status via RevenueCat (we do not receive your full
  card details; payment is handled by Apple/Google).
- **Diagnostics** — crash and error reports via Sentry, with personal data
  scrubbed.

We do **not** use third-party advertising and do **not** track you across other
apps or websites.

## How we use it

To provide the app's features (your blueprint, daily readings, Ask Soluna,
compatibility, tarot, journal, rituals), to manage your subscription, to keep the
app stable and secure, and to respond to support requests.

## Third parties (processors)

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and serverless functions.
- **RevenueCat** + **Apple/Google** — subscription management and payments.
- **Astrology data provider** and an **AI/LLM provider** — to compute charts and
  generate reflective text. We send only the data needed (e.g. birth details for
  a chart; your blueprint context for guidance). Raw journals and other people's
  full charts are not sent to the AI provider. Where supported, AI requests are
  routed to avoid training/retention.
- **Sentry** — diagnostics (PII-scrubbed).
- **Google Maps** — birthplace lookup during onboarding (server-side).

We do not sell your personal data.

## Storage, security, retention

Data is stored with Supabase and protected by row-level security so you can only
access your own records. We keep your data while your account exists.

## Your rights & choices

- **Access / export** — request a copy via `[support email]`.
- **Deletion** — delete your account in-app (Profile → Delete my account), which
  removes your data (cascading deletes). You may also email `[support email]`.
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights to correct, restrict, or
  object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a regulator.

## Children

Soluna is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in
your region). We do not knowingly collect their data.
